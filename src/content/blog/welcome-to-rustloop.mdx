# Welcome to RustLoop

We're building something here in the Rust Belt. Not the kind of thing that gets announced at a keynote in San Francisco — the kind that gets built in machine shops and university labs and living rooms, by people who know what it means to make something real.

## What We're About

RustLoop connects AI and machine learning practitioners across three cities: **Youngstown**, **Cleveland**, and **Pittsburgh**. We meet regularly, we share what we know, and we help each other get better.

No gatekeeping. No hype cycles. Just people doing the work.

## What to Expect

- **Monthly meetups** rotating between our three cities
- **Technical talks** from practitioners, not evangelists
- **Study groups** for papers, frameworks, and new tools
- **A community** that gives a damn about this region

## Get Involved

Whether you're training models at work, tinkering with LLMs on weekends, or just curious about where this field is heading — you belong here.

Stay tuned. We're just getting started.
